Cooking Station requirement
---------------------------
- Add one solid Cooking Station, symbol K, on the farm. Actors cook from an adjacent tile.
- The station offers one action per known recipe; cooking costs no stamina.
- A failed recipe attempt spends the turn but consumes no items and changes nothing.
- Ingredients belong to one category only: Fruit, Vegetable, or Animal product. Categories
  and ingredients may grow in future; recipes must depend on categories, never concrete items.
- Every selected ingredient applies its own cooking effect once, then is removed. Dishes are
  not ingredients and cannot be cooked again.
- Recipe execution order: validate all ingredients; apply each ingredient effect and remove it;
  apply recipe cook effect; add a new dish object to the Farmer inventory.

Ingredient rules
----------------
- Melon (Fruit): 50% chance to burst while used; deals 15 damage to every actor adjacent to
  the station. It is consumed either way.
- Parsnip (Vegetable): creates a Parsnip Crop on one random adjacent Soil tile, if present.
- Egg (Animal product): 25% chance to hatch a Chicken on one random adjacent enterable tile,
  if present. It is consumed either way.
- Optional additional ingredients: Strawberry/Tomato as Fruit, Cauliflower as Vegetable, and
  Milk as Animal product. Each must have a distinct cooking effect.

Recipe rules
------------
- Fixed-amount recipes select exactly their stated counts by category.
- Uses-everything recipes select every carried ingredient in one category and require at least one.
- One ingredient may satisfy only one required slot.
- Jam (J): uses every Fruit. Its pulp changes each adjacent bare Dirt tile to Soil. Each Jam
  stores its own fruit count; eating it restores that many stamina points, capped at maximum.
- Omelette (e): needs one Animal product and one Vegetable. 30% chance to place Fire on one
  random adjacent tile (lifespan 2, burn duration 3, 5 damage per turn). Eating it removes all
  Farmer status effects, beneficial or harmful.
- Harvest Feast (H): needs 5 Fruit, 5 Vegetable, and 5 Animal product. A second feast is placed
  on one random adjacent tile; the Farmer receives one. Eating it increases maximum HP and
  stamina by 1 and fully restores both current values.

Recommended design vocabulary
-----------------------------
- Ingredient and IngredientCategory.
- Recipe abstraction, with FixedAmountRecipe and UsesEverythingRecipe specialisations.
- JamRecipe, OmeletteRecipe, and HarvestFeastRecipe.
- CookingStation and CookAction.
- Dish base type with Jam, Omelette, and HarvestFeast subclasses.
- Keep category selection, ingredient effects, recipe effects, and dish consumption effects
  separate to preserve SOLID and DRY principles.
